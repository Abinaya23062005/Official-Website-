How long does it take to build a website?+
A simple portfolio or landing page takes 2–4 days. A full-stack web application with backend and database takes 1–2 weeks depending on complexity. I'll give you a clear timeline before starting.
Do you teach absolute beginners?+
Yes! I teach students from scratch with zero programming knowledge. My classes are project-based and beginner-friendly. I've taught school students from 9th grade all the way to college final year students.
What do DSA classes cover?+
Arrays, Strings, Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues, Trees, Graphs, Sorting, Searching, Dynamic Programming, and more. Classes are tailored based on your level — beginner, intermediate, or placement-focused.
Can you integrate AI into my existing website?+
Yes! I work with OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Groq APIs. I can add chatbots, document summarisers, content generators, or any AI feature to your existing web app.
Do you offer online classes?+
Yes, all classes are available online via Google Meet or Zoom. I'm based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu but teach students across India.
